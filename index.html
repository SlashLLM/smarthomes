For owners

The honest answers to the things that keep you up at night.

What about regulations? Council, Healthy Homes, the lot.

+

We handle the complete compliance stack: council STR registration where required (Auckland, Queenstown-Lakes, Rotorua, Christchurch), Healthy Homes warrant of fitness, smoke alarm and carbon monoxide testing, and meth-contamination baseline testing. We're a registered operator with every council we work in, and we keep your paperwork in your Owner Portal.

  • • Council STR registration filed within 7 days of onboarding
  • • Healthy Homes WoF refreshed annually
  • • Baseline and post-tenancy meth tests at our cost

What about wear and tear? Guests can be hard on a place.

+

Every stay ends with a professional clean and a checklist inspection. Linens are hotel-grade and rotated, and any damage is logged with photos before the next guest arrives. We carry $5M public liability and a $2,500 per-stay guest damage policy — plus a $1,000 SmartHomes top-up guarantee on every booking.

What about vacancy? Won't it just sit empty in winter?

+

Our pricing engine adjusts every 24 hours across six platforms (Airbnb, Booking, Bookabach, VRBO, Expedia, Google Vacation Rentals). Average annual occupancy across our portfolio is 83%; in shoulder seasons we lean into mid-stay corporate and relocation guests to keep yields steady.

Can I still use my own property?

+

Absolutely. Block any dates from your Owner Portal — instant, no questions. Most owners reserve 4–8 weeks a year for themselves and friends. If you let us know early we can usually slot you into a low-demand window with zero income impact.

What are the fees, really?

+

A flat 18% of nights booked. No setup fee, no listing fee, no monthly minimum. Cleaning, linen and consumables are passed through at cost and billed to the guest. You see the maths line by line on your monthly statement.

How do I get paid, and when?

+

Direct deposit on the 5th of every month, in NZD, to any NZ or Australian bank account. We can split payouts across multiple accounts (handy for trusts and co-ownership). Average clearance is 4 days from guest checkout.

What happens if I want to leave?

+

Thirty days written notice, full stop. We hand back the photography, the calendar, the guest reviews — all yours. We'd rather you stay because we're earning it, not because we've trapped you.

Is my property right for SmartHomes?

+

Generally yes if it's a self-contained whole home (not a room in a shared house), within 90 minutes of an airport or visitor centre, and free of body-corp short-stay restrictions. The free assessment is the fastest way to know — we'll tell you straight if it isn't a fit.
